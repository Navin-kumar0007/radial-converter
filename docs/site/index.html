Named for what you're trying to do

Make it fit
Slices like WhatsApp, Email and Under 100 KB. Say what the file is for, not which settings it needs.
Keep going
The result appears at your cursor, so you can drag it straight into Gmail, WhatsApp Web or an upload box. On a phone, share it.
Form photos
The exact pixels and KB an exam or job form asks for. Frame it yourself, then get a JPEG inside the limits. Limits stay editable, because forms change them.
History and undo
Originals are never touched. On a Mac or Windows PC, change your mind and the result goes to the Trash, where you can still get it back.
Crop, trim, merge
See the real frames while trimming a video. Merge, split, rotate and compress PDFs. Open locked PDFs and ZIPs with their password.
Whole folders
Drop a folder of up to 200 files. A damaged file gets its own error, and the rest still finish.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid version, no ads, no account. The code is open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.

Why does my computer warn me about it?

Macs and Windows PCs warn about apps that haven't paid for or been granted a signing certificate, and this one doesn't have one yet. The steps above get past it, and the checksums let you confirm you have the real file.

Does it work with no internet?

Yes. The apps never need it, and the web app works offline once it has been opened once.

How big can a file be?

Very large videos work, as they're read piece by piece. Pictures, PDFs and archives work in memory, up to 1 GB across a job.

What doesn't it do yet?

PDF to Word keeps text and headings, but not yet pictures on text pages, tables or exact layout, and scans can't be edited. Locked 7z and RAR files and RARs in several parts can't be opened. Passwords can be added to archives, but not to PDFs.

Will a form accept my photo?

It will have the size the form asks for, but forms can refuse a photo for other reasons, and their rules change. Always check your form's own notice. The app only crops and scales.

Something's wrong. Who do I tell?

Please open an issue on GitHub.
